OUR STORY

The people
behind the name.

Two friends. A restaurant on Randleman Road.
And a place in the life of the neighborhood.

01

Frank Millikan
& Larry Luck

Friends behind the counter.

Frank Millikan and Larry Luck were friends and fellow owner-operators of Frank & Larry’s. Their connection to the area went well beyond the restaurant.1 2

Frank coached in the Randleman and Level Cross Little Leagues for years. Larry coached local baseball, too, and gave his time to community organizations. Their story belongs to the neighborhood as much as it belongs to the name on the sign.

THE PEOPLE MADE THE PLACE

Remembering Sherry.

Larry’s wife, Sherry Luck, spent many hours here alongside him. She is remembered for her warmth toward children and her affection for the people she came to know at the restaurant.3

Those everyday connections are part of Frank & Larry’s history, too: people coming in for a meal and mattering to the people who were here.

A LITTLE LOCAL RACING HISTORY

A hot dog with
“The King.”

Richard Petty has a soft spot for the hot dogs here. In a 2026 Garden & Gun interview, he named Frank & Larry’s when asked where visitors should eat.4

“Super hot dogs.”

Richard Petty · Garden & Gun, 2026

Richard and his son Kyle also sat down for hot dogs here on Dinner Drive with Kyle Petty. You’ll find their visit in season one, episode six.5
