A simple visit.
A simple privacy note.

This website concept provides restaurant information, menu browsing, photos, phone links, and directions. It does not take orders, accept payments, or provide an account or contact form.

Information on this website

The website code does not add advertising trackers, analytics, or tracking cookies. Menu searches stay in your browser; they are not sent to the restaurant. This public concept is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs.

Links to other services

Directions open Google Maps. Story sources open other publishers’ websites. Those services’ own privacy terms apply. Phone links open your device’s calling application. No call is placed automatically.

Business questions

For menu or restaurant questions, call (336) 674-9177. This notice and the business details should be reviewed by the restaurant owner before any official restaurant launch.
