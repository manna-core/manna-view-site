Before you ask

The useful details.

How long does a website take?

The target is 7–10 business days after the deposit, approved content, and account access are ready. We agree the actual schedule before starting. The founding projects are built one at a time.

Do I need to buy monthly care?

No. You can buy the website on its own. The $79 monthly option covers the checks and small edits listed above; ordinary requests are acknowledged within two business days.

Can you work on my existing website?

Often, yes. We first check the platform, access, and the specific change. A small repair gets its own quote; it does not automatically need a replacement site.

What falls outside the $599 package?

Online stores, payments, customer accounts, custom booking, CRM integrations, sensitive-data intake, paid ads, ongoing SEO campaigns, social posting, branding, and photography are outside this starting package. Any different scope needs its own agreement.

Who makes the website?

Grayson runs Manna Agency and is your point of contact. AI tools assist with research, drafting and implementation; Grayson reviews the work and owns the commitments. Your business facts and assets are approved by you.

What happens after launch?

You receive source files and a handoff. Defects against the accepted scope are corrected for fourteen calendar days after launch. New requests are quoted separately, or may fit optional monthly care.
