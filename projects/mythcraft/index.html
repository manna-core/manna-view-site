Mythcraft · In development

A world built by the people who live in it.

A Minecraft project centered on professions, building, trade, and towns.

Ask about this project →

The idea

The idea is a world where making useful things gives people a reason to work together. Gatherers supply materials, makers create goods, and builders turn shared effort into places.

What it explores

  • Ten cumulative profession paths in development
  • Material-backed building and physical trade
  • A Java and Bedrock direction with familiar Minecraft play

Where it stands

In development

A development candidate, not a public server launch. Live player experience, balance and crossplay still need testing. No joining address or launch date is announced.
