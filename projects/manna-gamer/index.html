Manna-Gamer · Research prototype

Learning to play from what it can see.

A local game-playing AI research project built around pixels, bounded actions, and replay.

Ask about this project →

The idea

The current work uses a controlled training world to study navigation and recovery. Each move starts from a visible observation and ends with a fresh check of what happened.

What it explores

  • Pixel-based observations
  • Small actions with stop and recovery behavior
  • Recorded traces for reviewing progress

Where it stands

Research prototype

Training-world movement and chained missions have been tested. This is not a general game-playing product; live-game transfer remains a separate milestone.
