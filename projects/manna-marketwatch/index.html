Manna-MarketWatch · Private research lab

A research desk that keeps its evidence.

The idea

The project organizes observations, notes and replay evidence so research can be reviewed later. It treats an interesting result as something to inspect, not a reason to promise returns.

What it explores

  • Watchlists and manual snapshots
  • Paper-trade journaling
  • Replay studies with evidence attachments

Where it stands

Private research lab

Private research only. No investment advice, order routing, or profitability claim.
