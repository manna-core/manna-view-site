Manna Snips · Released · Windows

From screen to clipboard. Without the clutter.

A small screenshot utility for capturing, marking up, and pasting what matters.

Download Manna Snips ↗

The idea

Take a region screenshot, add an arrow or highlight, then copy it into a message or document. Download is the explicit action that saves a lasting PNG.

What it explores

  • Region capture and configurable hotkeys
  • Arrows, boxes, pen and highlights
  • Clipboard-first export and local settings

Where it stands

Released · Windows

Windows release v0.1.1. A focused screenshot tool, with no cloud account or telemetry.
