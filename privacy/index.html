Privacy, plainly.

This site introduces our work. It does not ask you to create an account.

Last updated September 30, 2026.

Browsing

We do not add advertising trackers or visitor analytics to the main site. GitHub Pages provides hosting and may process technical request information. Google Fonts provides the fonts. Those providers receive network requests when you visit.

Getting in touch

Email links open your own email app. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send through Proton Mail and use it to respond and discuss the work. Please do not send passwords or sensitive customer information.

The landscape quote form

The separate landscape page uses FormSubmit. Submitting it sends the details you enter to that provider for delivery to our landscape inbox; its anti-spam flow may involve additional providers. Direct phone and email options are also available.

The Morrow House demo

The fictional shop’s inquiry preview stays in the page’s memory. It sends no message and clears when you reload. Use made-up details when trying it.

Questions

Contact dev@manna-core.dev about information you sent us or to request its removal. Any future client project has its own agreed data-handling scope.
