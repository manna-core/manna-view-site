This site introduces Grayson’s work. The fictional task demo stores its tasks in your browser.

Updated November 24, 2026.

Browsing

We add no advertising trackers or visitor analytics to the main site. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process ordinary technical requests. The main pages use system fonts; separate older demonstrations or studies may load their own assets/providers.

Contact

Email links open your own email app. If you email us, we receive what you choose to send through Proton Mail and use it to discuss the work. Please do not send passwords, payment details or customer records. Correspondence is retained as needed to respond and manage agreed work; you can contact us about removal.

The fictional task demo

The public work sample uses deterministic rules, not a live language model or MCP service. Fictional tasks are stored only in this browser’s IndexedDB. They are not synced across devices, sent to a task server or shared with other visitors. This browser version is separate from the original Python/SQLite example.

Reload can retain tasks, but there is no backup or guaranteed retention. Private browsing, browser cleanup, storage limits or clearing site data can remove them. Use Clear demo tasks and confirm to remove this demo’s tasks from this browser. Closing a tab does not necessarily delete them. Use fictional information only.

The website demonstration

Morrow House is a fictional shop. Its form previews input in page memory and sends no inquiry. Reload clears that preview; use made-up details. Independent business studies are not official business sites or client endorsements.

The separate landscape form

The landscape page uses FormSubmit. Sending its form provides the information you enter to that provider for delivery to our landscape inbox; its anti-spam flow may involve other providers. A thank-you page cannot confirm inbox delivery. You can use its direct contact options instead. No agency inquiry form is active on the main site.
